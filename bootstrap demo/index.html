Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

What Are AI Tools?

Artificial Intelligence, also known as AI, is changing the way people learn, work, communicate, and solve problems. AI tools can help users generate ideas, write content, answer questions, analyze information, and improve productivity.

This website introduces three popular AI assistants: ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini. Each tool offers different features that can help people with everyday tasks, education, research, and professional work.

Benefits of Using AI Tools
  • Save time when completing everyday tasks.
  • Generate ideas and improve creativity.
  • Assist with writing and communication.
  • Support learning and research.
  • Help explain complicated information.

Although AI tools can be helpful, users should review the information they provide because AI responses may sometimes contain errors.
